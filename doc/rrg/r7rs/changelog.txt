The following logs cases in which I've chosen to change, rather than emulate,
the official R7RS content:

- Section 1.3.1: "The full list all the standard libraries and the identifiers
  they export is given in Appendix A." -> "The full list of all the standard
  libraries and the identifiers they export is given in Appendix A."

- Section 3.5: "A formal definition of proper tail recursion can be found in
  [6]." -> "A formal definition of proper tail recursion can be found in Proper
Tail Recursion and Space Efficiency." (plus a footnote)

- Change all uses of two-l "labelled" to "labeled" (both are used in R7RS)

- Move double-quote characters from the right to the left of
  sentence-ending punctuation, like "this".

- "<Clauses>" -> "<Clause>s" in section 4.2.1 (R7RS uses both forms)

- Different alignment of example results when they look odd in the R7RS pdf

- Section 4.2.6: "This sections" -> "This section"

- Section 1.3.4: Add explanation of notation used to show printed
  output (for info/plaintext formats only)
